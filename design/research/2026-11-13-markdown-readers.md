# Short research: Markdown readers and the Current reference

13 November 2026. Short research approved by Matteo in Waggle (case c_ed9ba7bf).

## Current (Matteo's screenshot, main reference)

Source: the screenshot Matteo attached (`~/.t3/userdata/attachments/mcp-launch-…-526571c0….png`).

- The window has two materials. The sidebar and the frame let the desktop background show through (vibrancy).
  The content sits in an almost black panel, inset about 8 px from the edge, with corners of about 10 px.
- The sidebar has small icons, 13 px text and the count right-aligned in gray.
  The selected row has a white fill at about 10 %.
- In the list, each row has a white 13 px title and a truncated gray detail line, with the time on the right.
- The document uses a column of about 330 px in the center panel, 15 px text and wide line spacing. Headings
  are semibold and not huge.
- The toolbar has no background of its own: only the breadcrumb and the "1 / 7" pagination.
- There are no accent colors, except the status dots. A calm app.

## iA Writer

Source: https://ia.net/writer (read on 13/11).

- The writing screen has no buttons: "a white rectangle".
- Focus mode highlights the current sentence or paragraph and fades the rest. This is the idea
  we take with Rune's flashlight effect.
- Source and preview can sit side by side. Markdown headings hang outside the margin (`#` in the left
  margin).

## Bear

Source: https://bear.app (read on 13/11). The page says little about typography.

- Three columns: sidebar, note list, editor. The sidebar has a table of contents of the headings.
- Markdown is formatted as you type. It has 28 themes or more.

## Obsidian

Not read: the help page on view modes returns 404. From what I know (assumption, not checked

It is the same key we chose.

## What we take

1. From Current: the two materials, that is a translucent frame and a solid content panel, black or white.
2. From iA: reading without buttons and focus on the paragraph.
3. From Bear and Obsidian: the table of contents of the headings and Cmd+E.
4. The effects Matteo chose in the Rune gallery: liquid metal, motion, fire, electric, skeleton, dither
   and flashlight. Each one must have a job, not be decoration only.
