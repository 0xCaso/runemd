# The forge at night

A rich page for the Full forge directions: every block the seven directions dress, in reading
order, with [a link to the decision](https://example.com/decision) and `inline code`.

> [!NOTE]
> The edges are still: drawn once per frame, they never hold the display link.

> [!TIP]
> Turn one direction on with `RUNEMD_FORGE=glass`, or all with `RUNEMD_FORGE=all`.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Off by default: without the switch the page is the one you know.

> [!WARNING]
> The charged edge moves with the metal's 8 wake-ups a second.

> [!CAUTION]
> Do not touch the anvil while it glows.
